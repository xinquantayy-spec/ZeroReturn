# Art direction: low-fidelity observational realism

## Keywords
Early-2000s field-recording camera, cold sea air, utilitarian industrial concrete, soft digital sensor noise, worn canvas, 256px textiles, chunky deliberate polygon silhouettes, grounded matte materials, abandoned shipping infrastructure, mixed overcast and sodium orange lighting. Nothing occult, monster-based or excessively dark. Mood is lonely and tense, but visibility is fair.

## Distinguishing from generic Roblox
- Original Blender-authored operators and props; no default rounded avatar head, printed smiling face, Toolbox soldiers or default gun grips.
- Environment dimensions grounded in believable architecture; avoid overly thick doors or enormous stair risers.
- Differentiated roughness via baked hand-painted texture, not expensive dynamic PBR; recognizable silhouettes at 20% resolution.
- UI inspired by a small field recorder, not Roblox default inventory/menu. No rainbow gradients, anime fonts, rounded neon buttons, loot rarity beams, huge numbers floating over heads.
- Sound is functional; distant clang and footsteps give useful awareness.
- Texture art may be grainy, but visual motion should remain stable on phone.

## Palette cards
`#33383B` dark metal, `#555A56` wet concrete, `#B1A893` dust/sand, `#96A4A2` sea mist, `#8A6252` oxidation, `#C07946` faded warning, `#D6DBC9` instrument text.

## Must-have signature compositions
1. Spawning from a ferry service tunnel under massive rusted container stacks, pale sea sky behind.
2. Pump Annex turbine room seen from a low mezzanine, clanking pipe and single orange control light.
3. Exiting at emergency radio beacon by rain-specked gate, visible relay tower in distance.

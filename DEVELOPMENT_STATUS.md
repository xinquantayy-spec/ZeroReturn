# Zero Return: Development Status

Build `0.10.0-audit`. Last updated 2026-11-09.

Zero Return is an original extraction shooter for Roblox. The core loop is inspired by Tarkov and the camera language by BODYCAM. Maps copy Tarkov's layouts with our own buildings and names (owner, 2026-11-01). Playable: **Border Yard** (Customs' layout at real scale) and **Meridian Mall** (Interchange's), **Smelter** (Factory's layout at real scale since 2026-11-09, art-1a), and **Quay 7**, an industrial port of our own that art-1a keeps as the test map. **Pinewatch** (woods-type) is built but parked by the owner (2026-10-29), kept in the repo and out of the map vote.

**Standing rule (owner, 2026-10-31):** develop Zero Return as close as possible to Escape from Tarkov in all aspects, less the exceptions in `docs/design/EFT_PARITY.md` (IP, the owner's design choices, Roblox limits, cross-platform). Research before each task, parity check before moving on. The parity gap list (item information, quality of life, controls, settings, visuals) is in that file.

## How a raid works (M7)

1. **Deploy** at the hideout terminal as a **PMC** (your gear) or a **player scav** (free random kit, 10-minute cooldown).
2. If no raid is running, the first deploy starts a **12-second matching** countdown; everyone who deploys meanwhile goes in together. Squad members insert at the same point.
3. The raid lasts as long as its map's Tarkov counterpart, the same for everyone: **40 minutes** on Border Yard and Meridian Mall, **20** on Smelter (Factory) and **25** on Quay 7. PMCs can still join during the **first 5 minutes**, scavs until **6 minutes are left**; otherwise you queue for the next raid.
4. Exfils depend on your insertion side. Some cost money (PIER 3 BOAT, 8,000 MK), need a key (SERVICE SHAFT, office key), may be closed for the whole raid (CRANE YARD LIFT), or are scav-only (STORM CULVERT). Press **O** to see yours.
5. When the clock runs out, anyone still inside is **missing in action**. The raid also ends once nobody is left inside. Bodies and dropped gear are cleared, and **insured** items that nobody extracted go to their insurer, who sends them back hours later in the messenger (EFT's windows divided by 12 since gameplay-6b: VOS 2-3 h, HALE 1-2 h, BRAM 10-20 min).

## Progression and economy (M9)

- **Traders** (relay radio in the hideout): VOS (weapons, magazines, ammunition, attachments), HALE (medicine and food), TILDA (armour, rigs, packs) and the FENCE (buys anything at a poor rate; sells a few odds and ends, including the office key). Each has loyalty levels 1–4 that need a player level, money traded with them and standing (from tasks). Higher levels unlock better stock and are 5% cheaper per level. Stock is limited per 30-minute restock.
- **Selling:** right-click an item in the stash and choose SELL; the best-paying trader buys it. Items must be examined and containers empty. Quest items, secure containers and the recovery kit cannot be sold.
- **Tasks:** accept them at the trader, progress in raids (kills count even if you die later; extractions; visiting places), hand in **found-in-raid** items and collect the reward (money, XP, standing, items delivered to the stash).
- **XP and skills:** XP for kills (more for headshots, Watch, the Foreman and PMCs), looting, examining and surviving. The levels are EFT's table to level 79 since 2026-11-08 (economy-4b): level 15, the flea market, needs 177,337 XP; a raid lost to death or MIA keeps 75 % of its experience; task experience keeps each task's old share of a level (`Progress.luau`, `QuestData.luau`). 24 of Tarkov's skills in four groups level with use (2026-11-01), among them endurance (sprint drain), strength (weight limits), vitality (bleed chance) and metabolism (energy/water drain).
- **Run-through:** extracting within 7 minutes with less than 200 raid XP keeps the loot but nothing becomes found-in-raid.
- **First raid** (since 2026-11-08, ui-5a): Tarkov 1.0's tutorial raid on a course of its own, offered to a profile that has never raided (START, LATER, SKIP): a loaned kit, eight steps on an objective line (meds at the reception desk, the SMG from a body, loading it, clearing the garage, first aid, the street, the tents, the gate under a green flare), control signs for the device in use and four leashed scavs; the loot goes to the stash found in raid. One run at a time per server.
- **Hideout** (H, the menu bar or a bunker bay; since 2026-11-08, economy-7b; every one of the 14 modules has its station and a stencil with your level in Relay 7 since the same evening): EFT's 14 core modules with EFT 1.1's levels, costs, prerequisites and bonuses, build and craft times divided by 4 (also while offline and in raids); several builds at once, one craft per module; fuel and power (a canister lasts 3 h 9 min; without power, powered crafts will not start and crawl); the bitcoin farm, the scav case and 83 of EFT's current crafts (the other 129 listed with the reason each is out); the stash 10x30 to 10x68. Details and gaps in `docs/design/EFT_PARITY.md` section 10.
- **Scav karma:** killing local scavs as a player scav lowers karma; extracting as one raises it. High karma shortens the scav cooldown and improves the Fence's prices; below −1 the locals shoot your scav on sight.
- **Dog tags:** a dead PMC's body carries their dog tag (name, level, killer); tags are worth more at higher levels.
- **Keys:** the depot office is locked; the Harbor Office Key opens it for the rest of the raid; each unlock uses up one of its 25 uses.

## Raid world (M10)

- **Time and weather:** every raid rolls a start time and weather; in-world time runs at 7x, so a dusk raid ends in the dark. The deployment terminal shows the next raid's time and weather, and the REC stamp shows the raid clock.
- **Night:** the bodycam's auto-gain lifts the picture with more grain; street lamps switch on; weapon lights matter (and give you away). Guards and the Foreman's crew use theirs at night.
- **Weather:** fog shortens sight for everyone (AI included); rain dulls hearing, wets surfaces and lenses, and brings thunder with lightning in heavy storms.
- **Crew dormitory (Block B):** two storeys of crew rooms off an exterior balcony and stairs. Rooms 104 and 203 are locked (keys from raids or the Fence) and hold better loot.
- **Depot vault:** behind the locked harbor office; the Vault Keycard (10 uses) opens it at the card reader. Two safes, an arms crate and a shelf of valuables.
- **Loose loot:** items lie on desks, shelves, pallets and floors around the map; look at one and press F to take it (found-in-raid if you extract). Unexamined items show as UNKNOWN ITEM.
- **Doors:** AI open unlocked doors on their way; locked doors block AI paths until someone unlocks them; some doors start ajar.

## Architecture

- **Server (`src/server`)**
  - Main and Services share one `ctx` table.
  - Services: Remotes, Store, Profiles, Inv, Settle, Assets, World, DetailPlan, Chars, Appearance, Loot, Health, Combat, Grenades, Extract, AI, Raid, Match, Economy, Quests, Hideout, Doors, Dev.
  - Appearance builds every character's worn gear from the equipment slots and the look (`Equipment.resolve`), diffs it per slot, and reconciles fallbacks when a late asset batch arrives. Chars keeps the rig, weapon visuals and poses.
  - Grenades owns pin pulls and throws (validated acts), the authoritative path (shared `Grenades.simulate`), explosions (fragments through the health model, AI flashes) and smoke volumes that block AI sight.
  - Match rolls each raid's environment (shared `Env`); Loot also owns loose loot (spawned per raid from World.looseSpots, built from the item models in `ItemMeta`, worn gear from the character meshes).
  - AI owns AI units (perception, tactics, weapons) plus the spawn director and the cover-point map; units use the same rig, ballistics, health and loot as players.
  - Match owns the raid cycle (queue, matching, shared clock, entry windows, end conditions), squads, spawn picking and insurance settlement; Raid owns per-player sessions, deploy/enter/finalize and inventory requests.
  - Economy owns traders, selling, XP, skills and karma; Quests owns task state and progress; Hideout owns modules and crafts; Doors owns locked doors and keys.
  - Maps (`src/server/Maps`): one module per map; MapKit (walls, slabs, stairs, roofs) and TerrainKit are shared; `Kit.luau` places Blender kit props (pieces per material from `KitMeta.luau`, invisible box colliders, a plain box if the kit has not loaded). World keeps map switches (the mall generator) that Doors operates and Extract reads.
  - Everything that matters is decided on the server: shots and damage, inventory, loot, extraction, currency and profiles.
- **Persistence**
  - Session-locked profiles (UpdateAsync with a lock that expires after 150 s).
  - Loadouts are held in escrow at deploy and saved before the player spawns.
  - Raids are settled idempotently by raid ID.
  - An unresolved raid on load is settled as a DISCONNECTED loss.
- **Remotes**
  - One RemoteFunction (`Req`) plus Shoot, Act, State, Note and Fx.
  - Every request goes through token buckets, request-ID de-duplication, payload checks and strikes.
- **Client (`src/client`)**
  - Scriptable first-person bodycam camera (eye from the rig pose), Movement (stance, lean, stamina, weight, vault), Weapon (viewmodel from the shared spec, IK arms, optics), Animator (RigMath full-body animation of every operator), IAS input with a CAS fallback.
  - Look: Post (grain, vignette, edge haze, exposure, pain), Atmos (raid lighting, weather, rain, lamps, ambience) and Lens (HUD text and icons, straight by default) drive the recorder look.
  - Detail (M12) builds the cosmetic detail plan around the camera at the player's graphics level and handles POTATO (plain materials, no shadows).
  - Custom interaction prompts, grid inventory with item pictures (UiKit `itemIcon`), deploy, summary, settings and exits screens, trader and hideout terminals (Trade), touch cluster.
  - Grenade: throw input and first-person hands (through Weapon), every grenade in flight, explosions, stun flashes, smoke clouds, and the local blast effects (shake, concussion, white-out, deafness, smoke veil).

## Key decisions

The full log is in `docs/kit/STATE/DECISIONS.md`.

- **Name:** Zero Return. It was formerly "Escape From Bloxov"; the PC folder name is unchanged.
- **Rojo port:** 34873. Port 34872 belongs to another project.
- **Art:** procedural Blender scripts with vertex colours and no textures, uploaded through Open Cloud. The API key stays in the owner's home folder. The owner's pick art-4b adds PBR texture sets for hero assets: since 2026-11-09 every gun part wears them (48 sheets of colour, normal, roughness and metalness at 1024 px, `zr_tex.py`), the worn gear four and the block body two (`zr_gtex.py`, tinted per item and outfit through SurfaceAppearance.Color); every item lies in the world as its own model (375 item models, 2026-11-09); textures for items seen up close are next; bulk props and buildings stay vertex-coloured. The look rules: `docs/look/ART_BIBLE.md`.
- **Item pictures (M13):** rendered from the same Blender models as the loose loot and packed into two shared sheets (one image load for every picture), rather than live 3D views in the tiles.
- **Look 2.0:** worn surfaces are MaterialVariants on the server-built map (everyone sees them); clutter and grime are a client-side detail layer from a server plan, sized by the graphics setting, and never affect gameplay. The starting level is per device since 2026-11-07 (phones LOW, consoles MEDIUM, PCs HIGH; tech-3b); POTATO is opt-in.
- **Blender kit props (M13):** each prop is exported as one mesh per material sharing an origin, with no UVs: Roblox materials and MaterialVariants tile by studs on MeshParts (measured), so props get material detail with no texture memory of their own; shading, AO and grime are baked into vertex colours. Pieces never collide or take queries; a few invisible boxes per prop do. Indoor props cast no sun shadows, and depth of field is off under a roof.
- **Audio:** original synthesised sound effects, plus one ambience track from Roblox's licensed library.
- **Map:** one shared raid world per server, one raid at a time (Match). Each player has their own raid session inside it.
- **AI factions:** AI never fight each other (the Watch and the scavs keep a truce, so the map does not empty itself); AI scavs, the Foreman's group and most bosses leave player scavs alone unless provoked or their karma is low; the Watch, THE CARETAKER, THE CULT, MORTAR and KESTREL shoot them on sight (EFT's data for the bosses, audit ai.4; the Watch is ai-1a's to remove); everyone else is a target.
- **AI fairness:** reaction delays (0.45–1.5 s), a zeroing-in penalty for the first seconds of a fight, burst recoil, movement/suppression penalties and no wall-hacks (they see through glass gaps and doorways only).
- **Raid cycle:** 12 s matching, raids as long as the map's Tarkov counterpart (40 min on Border Yard and Meridian Mall, 20 on Smelter, 25 on Quay 7), PMC late entry 5 min (a compromise so nobody waits a full raid; gameplay-7a, a server per raid, removes it), scavs until 6 min left, early end when empty.
- **Squads:** no friendly markers or teammate health (Tarkov); squads wear one armband colour on both arms. The roster shows in the hideout and briefly at insertion or with O.
- **Bodies:** Roblox destroys a character on respawn, so a dead player's body is copied into `workspace.ZR_Corpses` and stays lootable until the raid ends.
- **Economy scope:** traders, barters, tasks, hideout and skills follow Tarkov's rules with original names, on a much smaller scale (`docs/design/EFT_PARITY.md`, section 10). The flea market (2026-10-31) has Tarkov's rules with simulated sellers and buyers, because a market of real players needs trading across servers (the owner's pick economy-3a: a real market with an AI floor). Trader stock limits are per player per 30-minute restock.
- **Run-through:** 7 minutes / 200 XP (Tarkov uses similar values), so quick dev extractions show the rule.
- **Characters (M16):**
  - Block-proportioned R15 on the existing Motor6D/RigMath pipeline: no skinned meshes and no second rig.
  - Gear is rigid Blender meshes welded to one body segment. Layers are fitted by scaling over the layers underneath, not with layered-clothing cages.
  - Outfits are looks; protection and storage are items.
  - The Foreman's armour is worn under his jacket and stays hidden (the brief allows internal plates).
- **HUD size:** text is authored at 1080p and scales with screen height to the power 0.6 (minimum 0.5), so small windows and phones keep readable numerals; everything stays in the screen margins.
